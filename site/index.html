Is the judge fair?

An AI judge can be swayed by things that have nothing to do with the argument. We judged the same debates again and again, listing the sides in different orders, then changed who speaks first, then swapped in a different judge model and reran the debates themselves. Treat every percentage as one draw, not a precise measurement.

Can the quote checker be fooled?

A quote counts as verified when its best match in the filing scores 90 or more out of 100. We tried it on real quotes, then on altered ones, then on quotes from the wrong company.
